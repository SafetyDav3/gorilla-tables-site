Build your table920-246-9567

Good questions.

Can't find yours? Call the shop at 920-246-9567 or send a note.

If you can run a bead, you can build it. Parts are laser-cut with interlocking tabs and slots that fixture the structure square, and every table ships with a step-by-step assembly guide with a picture for each step. One person can do it with two sawhorses, a few clamps and a set of U-bolts; most customers have a table standing in an afternoon.

Files are sent once your order is approved, usually the same business day. Kits typically cut and ship within 5 to 7 business days. Welded tables run 2 to 3 weeks depending on finish and queue; powder-coated builds add a few days. You get a tracking number the day it leaves Denmark.

Tables ship LTL freight on a skid, nationwide. If you don't have a forklift or dock, add liftgate delivery at checkout and the driver brings it down to the ground for you.

1/4" is flat, rigid and right for most fab and hobby work. Go 3/8" if you regularly hammer, press or build heavy weldments on your table, or you just want the extra mass in reserve. 1/2" is for the biggest frames and the hardest use. The designer shows the rated load for each.

Yes, that's the whole point. Type any size up to 60" x 120" into the designer and it prices it on the spot, with the same ribs, tabs and hole grid as the standard sizes. Need something bigger? Request a quote and we'll build it in sections that bolt together.

5/8" holes on a 2" grid is the standard and fits the common fixture blocks, clamps and stops. 16 mm on 50 mm and 28 mm on 100 mm are a click away in the designer if that is your tooling.

An engineering estimate of the evenly spread load the table carries, worked out from its own plate, ribs, legs and feet with a safety factor of 3 on the steel's yield strength and a sag limit of 1/16" between the legs. The designer shows which part sets the limit. It is not a certified rating.

A laser-ready DXF for every part, sorted by plate thickness, plus the customer drawing and the assembly guide. They're for cutting your own table; reselling the files or parts cut from them isn't allowed. See the file licence.

Every table comes with 4" locking polyurethane casters, leveling feet, or leveling casters that roll and then wind down onto a foot. Pick it in the designer.

A lower shelf: 3/16" plate with 1" lips, bolted to the stand on laser-cut clips. On a kit the bend lines are slotted so you can bend the lips by hand over a bench edge; on a welded table we bend it for you.
