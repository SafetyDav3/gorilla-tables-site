The difference is in the details.

A top that means it

1/4", 3/8" or 1/2" steel plate, laser-cut with 5/8" fixture holes on a precise 2" grid (or 16 mm on 50 mm, if that's your tooling). Flat where it counts, with clamping anywhere on the surface, and enough mass to shrug off years of grinding and hammer work.

Laser-cut plate top

Clamp from every edge

Perforated side skirts wrap the full perimeter, so fixture blocks, stops and clamps work on all four sides, not just the top. Square once, clamp everywhere, weld with both hands free.

Full-perimeter hole grid

Rigid by design

An interlocking ribbed substructure ties the top to heavy tube legs, resisting twist, sag and drum. It goes together square and it stays that way, on casters or planted on fixed feet. Every table carries a rated load worked out from its own plate and ribs.

Ribbed substructure

Cut and built in Wisconsin

Every table is laser-cut, formed and welded by the crew at Gerarden Fabrication in Denmark, Wisconsin. The same hands that build our custom commercial work build your table.
