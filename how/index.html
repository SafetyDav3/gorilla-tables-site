How a kit goes together.

One person can build it. The parts are tabbed and slotted so the structure fixtures itself square, and the top is pulled flat with U-bolts before anything is welded.

  1. Top plate face downOn a flat surface. Knock the slag off every part and check the tabs drop into their slots.
  2. Ribs inCross ribs into the top's slots, long ribs over them so the half-laps interlock. A straightedge across the rib edges shows any high spot.
  3. Clamp and tackClamp each rib to the top and put small tacks at the crossings and rib ends. Just enough to hold it for the flip.
  4. Flip onto horses and U-boltA U-bolt in every bay hooks under each rib, legs up through the fixture holes, plate and nuts on top. Run them down from the middle out until no light shows under a straightedge.
  5. Weld the topTack every tab, then weld the pockets in short stitches from the middle out, letting it cool between passes. The tabs sit just below the surface so the welds dress flush.
  6. Walls onFlip it back with the U-bolts still on, slide the walls over the rib ends, draw them in with bar clamps, check the diagonals, tack and weld.
  7. Leg plates, legs, feetU-bolts off, leg plates in the corners, weld out, bolt on the legs with casters or levelers, stand it up and lock the wheels.

Every kit ships with the full guide: an exploded picture for each step, the U-bolt sizes for your hole system, and the hardware list. You can preview it for any table in the designer ("Assembly plan").
