File licence.

What you may and may not do with a Gorilla Tables file set.

Draft for review by DeWitt LLP before the site goes live. Not yet in effect.

When you buy a file set from Gorilla Tables (Gerarden Fabrication & Design LLC), you receive laser-cutting files, drawings and an assembly guide for the table you designed. You are buying a licence to use them, not the design itself.

You may

  • Cut one table from the files for your own use, on your own equipment or at a shop you hire to cut it for you.
  • Keep a copy of the files for your records and cut replacement parts for that table.
  • Modify the files to suit your own table.

You may not

  • Resell, share, publish, post or give away the files, in whole or in part, modified or not.
  • Cut tables or parts from the files for sale, or for anyone other than yourself, without written permission from Gerarden.
  • Use the Gorilla Tables name, logo or etched marks on anything you make from the files.

Ownership

The table design, the designer software, the drawings and the guide are the property of Gerarden Fabrication & Design LLC and are protected by copyright. Each file set carries the order number it was made for.

No warranty on the result

The files describe parts that, cut and assembled as the guide shows, make a table like the one in the designer. Cutting accuracy, assembly and the flatness, strength and safety of the finished table are your responsibility.

If the licence is broken

The licence ends at once if you break these terms, and Gerarden may pursue the remedies available to it.
