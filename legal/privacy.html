Privacy.

What we collect when you use the site and the designer, and what we do with it.

Draft for review by DeWitt LLP before the site goes live. Not yet in effect.

What we collect

  • Designs. The designer runs in your browser. A design you are working on is saved in your browser's own storage so you can come back to it; it is not sent to us until you add it to the cart, request a quote or place an order.
  • Orders and quotes. Your name, contact details, shipping address and the design, so we can build, ship and bill it. Payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store card numbers.
  • Site use. Standard web server logs and basic analytics (pages viewed, browser type) to keep the site working and improve it.

How we use it

To fulfil orders, answer quotes, send order updates, and improve the site and the products. We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the carriers, payment processors and service providers needed to complete your order, and when the law requires.

Cookies

The site uses the browser storage and cookies needed for the designer, the cart and basic analytics. You can clear them in your browser at any time; a saved design will be cleared with them.

Your choices

Email sales@fabricationtables.com to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop marketing email.
