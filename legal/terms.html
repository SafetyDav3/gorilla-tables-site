Terms of sale.

How ordering, payment, shipping and returns work for Gorilla Tables products.

1. Who we are

Gorilla Tables is a brand of Gerarden Fabrication & Design LLC ("Gerarden", "we"), 3190 Water Division Rd · Denmark, WI 54208. These terms apply to every order placed through fabricationtables.com, by phone or by email.

2. Products

We sell (a) laser-cut parts kits, (b) fully welded tables and (c) digital file sets for a table you design on our site. Dimensions, weights, prices and load ratings shown on the site and in the designer are generated from your design at the time you view it and may change if the design or our prices change before you order.

3. Prices and payment

Prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax, freight and liftgate delivery, which are shown before you pay. Custom-size and custom-option prices are calculated by the designer from your design. Payment in full is due at the time of order unless we agree otherwise in writing. We may correct an obvious pricing error before accepting an order.

4. Custom work

Every table other than a standard size is built to your design. Once we have cut steel for a custom order it cannot be cancelled or returned, except for a defect in our workmanship.

5. Lead times and shipping

Lead times shown on the site are estimates. Products ship by LTL freight on a skid to the address you give us. Risk of loss passes to you when the carrier takes the shipment. Inspect the shipment before signing for it and note any damage on the delivery receipt; tell us within 48 hours of delivery so we can file a claim with the carrier.

6. Load ratings and use

Rated loads are engineering estimates for an evenly spread static load on a table standing on a flat floor, and are not certified ratings. Do not exceed them, do not use a table as a lifting point or a platform for people, and keep casters locked when working. Welding, grinding and heavy fabrication are hazardous; you are responsible for using the table safely.

7. Kits

A kit is a set of parts that you assemble and weld. The assembly guide describes one proven method; the flatness, strength and safety of the finished table depend on your work. Gerarden is not responsible for the result of assembly carried out by you or a third party.

8. Warranty

We warrant fully welded tables against defects in materials and workmanship for one year from delivery, and kit parts against defects in cutting for 30 days from delivery. The remedy is repair, replacement or refund of the affected item at our option. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, overloading, modification, corrosion of raw steel, or assembly by anyone other than Gerarden. Except as stated here, products are provided without any other warranty, express or implied, including merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

9. Returns

Standard-size kits and tables in unused, uncut and unwelded condition may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund less return freight and a 15% restocking charge. Custom sizes, custom options, powder-coated items and digital files are not returnable.

10. Digital files

File sets are licensed, not sold, under the File licence. They are delivered after we approve the order and are not refundable once delivered.
