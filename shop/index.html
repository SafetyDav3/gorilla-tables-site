The shop.

Gorilla Tables is a brand of Gerarden Fabrication & Design, a custom metal fabrication shop in Denmark, Wisconsin.

Built by hand. Designed with purpose.

Gerarden Fabrication builds custom staircases, commercial weldments and one-off pieces for people who notice the difference. The fixture tables started the way most good tools do: we needed one that was flat, rigid and didn't cost more than the welder next to it, so we built it. Then other shops started asking.

Every Gorilla table is laser-cut on our own 4 kW laser, formed and welded here, and checked flat before it leaves. The kits are cut on the same machine from the same files; the only difference is whose hands do the welding.
